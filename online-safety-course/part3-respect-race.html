Respect Online: Race, Hate and Bullying

Hong Kong is home to people of many backgrounds. Racist comments, jokes and memes hurt real people. Under Hong Kong's Race Discrimination Ordinance, publicly inciting racial hatred is unlawful, and doing it with threats of harm is a crime. When someone attacks you, how you respond matters too.

The rules

  1. Don't post, share, like or forward racist jokes, slurs or memes, even 'as a joke'.
  2. If someone is racist to you: don't reply, screenshot, block, report, and tell a parent or teacher.
  3. Never fight back by posting someone's address, school or photos. That's doxxing, a crime even if they started it.
  4. Don't join pile-ons. Be an upstander: support the person being targeted and report.
  5. Angry? Wait before you post. Nothing online is truly deleted.
