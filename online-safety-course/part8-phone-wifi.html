Your Phone, Wi-Fi and Devices

Your phone holds your messages, photos, accounts and sometimes payment apps. Whoever holds it unlocked can act as you. Free Wi-Fi is handy but can be faked. A few habits keep both safe.

The rules

  1. Don't hand your unlocked phone to a stranger. If someone needs a call, point them to staff, security or the police, or make the call yourself on speaker.
  2. Never lend your phone, SIM card, accounts or bank account to anyone, not even friends.
  3. Lock your phone with a PIN or Face/Touch ID and a short auto-lock.
  4. On public Wi-Fi: check the network name with staff, avoid logging in or paying, forget the network afterwards, and turn off auto-join.
  5. Set AirDrop and Bluetooth to contacts only or off; install apps only from the official app store; keep your phone updated.
  6. Keep Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) switched on.
