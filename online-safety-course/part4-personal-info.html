Personal Information and Doxxing

Your address, school, phone number and daily routine are the keys to your real life. Sharing them, yours or anyone else's, can put people in danger. In Hong Kong, sharing someone's personal data to harm them is a crime called doxxing.

The rules

  1. Keep your address, school, phone number and schedule out of chats, bios and posts.
  2. Watch your photos: uniforms, building names and street signs give away where you are.
  3. Turn off location sharing in apps and on photos.
  4. Never post anyone else's personal information, ever. Doxxing in HK can mean up to HK$1,000,000 and 5 years' jail.
  5. Share passwords only with your parents. Never share verification codes.
  6. Post holiday photos after you're home, not live.
