Getting Help and Your Digital Footprint

Everyone makes mistakes online. What matters most is telling someone quickly, and remembering that what you post can follow you for years.

The rules

  1. Family rule: you will never be in trouble for telling us something went wrong online. Hiding it is what makes things worse.
  2. Something makes you uncomfortable? Stop, leave, tell a parent.
  3. Before posting, ask: would I be OK if my parents, teachers or a future university saw this?
  4. Deleted isn't gone. Screenshots and copies remain.
  5. Emergency or threats to your safety: call 999.
  6. Report and block instead of arguing.
