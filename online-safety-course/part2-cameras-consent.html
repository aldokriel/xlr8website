Cameras, Bodies and Consent

A person's body and image belong to them. In Hong Kong, recording people in private situations or sharing intimate images without consent are crimes, and images of anyone under 18 are treated very seriously everywhere.

The rules

  1. Never take photos or videos in bathrooms, toilets or changing rooms. Not of others, not of yourself.
  2. Never screenshot or record a video call where someone is undressed, even partly, even as a joke.
  3. Ask before taking, keeping or posting a photo of someone. Taking, keeping and sharing are three separate yeses.
  4. Never send a photo of yourself without clothes, to anyone, for any reason.
  5. If you receive a private image of someone: don't forward it, delete it, tell a trusted adult.
  6. On video calls: be fully dressed and in a shared room, not the bathroom.
