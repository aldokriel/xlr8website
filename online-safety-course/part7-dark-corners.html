Dark Corners: Extreme Groups, 'Challenges' and the Dark Web

In August 2026 police in Malmesbury, South Africa arrested a 16-year-old accused of links to '764', an international network police describe as violent online extremists who exploit children. According to reports, it ran on ordinary apps (Telegram, Discord, Snapchat and Instagram), not just the dark web. The FBI says these groups befriend young people, get a picture, then use it as blackmail to push them into worse and worse things, including hurting themselves or others. Teens get pulled in as victims, and some end up as participants facing criminal charges.

The rules

  1. Leave any group, server or chat that shares gore, cruelty, hate or 'shock' content. Don't save or share anything. Tell a parent.
  2. Never do a 'task', 'challenge' or 'dare' set by people online, especially anything involving hurting yourself, animals or anyone else, or filming it.
  3. No secret groups, invite-only servers or apps your parents don't know about.
  4. Don't download Tor or try to visit the dark web, not even 'just to look'. It's full of illegal markets, malware and content that is a crime to view or download.
  5. Staying silent in a group that harms someone makes you part of it. Leave and report.
  6. If you're already in too deep or being threatened: tell a parent. You will be helped, not blamed.
