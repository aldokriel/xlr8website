For parents

  • Do one part per sitting, ideally watching the videos together.
  • After each quiz, ask your teen to show you the certificate and talk through any questions they got wrong.
  • Progress is saved in this browser on this device only. Each child should use their own device, or clear progress between them.
  • Preview the Part 5 and Part 7 links first. Part 7's two news articles about the Malmesbury case are for parents; tell your teens about it in your own words.
  • The legal points are simplified for teaching, not legal advice.
