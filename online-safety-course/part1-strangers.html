Strangers, DMs and Invites

Most people online are not who you'd expect. Photos, names, ages and blue ticks can all be faked. The safest online circle is people you know in real life.

The rules

  1. Only chat with people you know in real life, or that your parents have met.
  2. Don't message random people, and don't DM celebrities. Accounts that reply are often impersonators.
  3. Decline follow requests, friend requests, group-chat and server invites from strangers.
  4. Never move a chat to another app (WhatsApp, Telegram, Snapchat) because someone online asked.
  5. Never meet someone from online in person without a parent.
  6. If anyone asks you to keep your chats secret, that's the moment to tell a parent.
