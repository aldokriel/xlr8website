ALDOMETER Speed

High-precision athletic speed sensor

Designed for sprinters, coaches, and track athletes who demand real-time data without the setup hassle. ALDOMETER Speed delivers millisecond-accurate velocity feedback straight to a high-visibility LED display.

No smartphone required, no heavy equipment, no wearables, and no complicated calibration — just power it on, run, and read your speed.

How it works

  1. Switch the device on.
  2. Place it anywhere on the track.
  3. Run past the device.
  4. See your speed on the display.

Features

  • Instant actual speed measurement — no averages, only instant
  • KPH / MPH / MPS setting
  • 3-stage speed gate settings for busy tracks
  • Accurate speed measurement to 0.1 km/h
  • Battery percentage indicator
  • Rugged design for outdoor tracks
  • Lightweight at only 251g (292g in the protective bag)
  • Portable — no tripods or cables
  • 5m optimal reading range
  • Big display, visible day and night from 20m away
  • Up to 10 hours of standby readings
  • USB-C charging
