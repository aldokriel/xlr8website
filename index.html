Flagship product

ALDOMETER Speed

ALDOMETER Speed is a portable radar-based speed measurement device built for sprint training and testing. Point it down the lane, and it reads out live speed and split data as the athlete runs — no wearables, no track markers, no waiting for the footage to sync.

It's designed for the environments where sprint training actually happens: outdoor tracks, gyms, and testing days with limited setup time.
