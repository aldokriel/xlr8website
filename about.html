About XLR8

Built by people who train on the same track they design for.

XLR8 Limited is a Hong Kong-based sports technology company developing precision timing and measurement hardware for sprinters and coaches, under the ALDOMETER brand.

Our story

XLR8 started from a simple frustration: sprint training generates almost no usable data. Stopwatches are imprecise, video review is slow, and most measurement tools are built for labs, not lanes.

We set out to build instruments that give athletes and coaches real numbers, on the spot, without changing how a training session actually runs. ALDOMETER Speed — our radar-based speed measurement device — is the first result of that work, engineered and refined through the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks (HKSTP) incubation programme.

Read the full story behind ALDOMETER on aldometer.com.

How we build

XLR8's team combines hardware engineering with real experience on the track, so every product decision is grounded in both what a device needs to survive and what a coach actually needs to see.

Just as important is what happens after a device ships. Feedback from the coaches and athletes using ALDOMETER in daily training feeds directly back into our design process — shaping firmware updates, feature priorities, and the products we build next.

What guides the work

Athlete & coach feedback

Every comment, every recommendation, every interaction is captured to shape and refine accuracy and usability, on the track.

Accuracy first

Every device is built to give a number you can trust and act on — not an approximation.

Track-ready

If it can't survive a real training session — heat, dust, a dropped case — it doesn't ship.

Athlete-first

Every product is designed around what helps the athlete improve, not what's easiest to build.

Where we're headed

ALDOMETER Speed is the foundation of a broader line of sprint training and measurement tools. We're building toward a fuller picture of a sprinter's performance — from the start, through the run, to the finish — and welcome conversations with investors, distributors, and facility partners who want to be part of that.
